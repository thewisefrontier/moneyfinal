Quarterly values are standalone three-month figures from 10-Q filings, and Q4 is the 10-K annual value minus the nine-month year-to-date total. Cash flows are computed from differences in year-to-date values. Q4 EPS is not in quarterly filings (10-Q), so it is shown as “-”. ROE and ROA divide the sum of the last four quarters of net income by quarter-end equity and assets. Because fiscal year-ends differ, quarters are labeled by quarter-end date instead of quarter name.
