How to read · Korean figures come from DART consolidated statements (key accounts) and US figures from SEC 10-K and 10-Q data. Quarterly comparison is available for roughly the top 600 Korean companies by market cap and for all US companies. Because currencies differ, compare Korean companies with Korean ones, or US with US. In each row, the blue bold figure is simply the highest among the companies compared (the lowest for debt ratio), not a judgment of good or bad. Margins and debt ratios differ by industry, so cross-industry comparisons are hard, and financial companies report revenue and debt differently from other firms. Companies with different fiscal year-ends are aligned by fiscal year (the year of the year-end date). For Korean companies, net income, ROE, PER and PBR use net income attributable to owners of the parent and parent equity (consolidated totals where unavailable). PER and PBR are shown only for Korean stocks, using today’s market cap. This is reference material, not investment advice.
