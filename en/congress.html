Notice · This data comes from periodic transaction reports (STOCK Act PTRs) filed with the Clerk of the US House of Representatives. US law (5 U.S.C. §13107) prohibits using these reports for commercial purposes, soliciting money, or credit ratings. MoneyFinal carries no ads or donation requests on this page. Reports can be published up to 45 days after a trade, and amounts are ranges, not exact values. Senate trades are not included. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security, and a member’s trade does not guarantee later returns.
Data basis · Source: the House Clerk’s financial disclosure system (disclosures-clerk.house.gov). We read and organized the transaction report (PTR) PDFs, and each row’s "original"lets you check the filing directly. Owners are the member, spouse (SP), joint (JT) and dependent child (DC). Sell (partial) means part of the holding was sold. Filings published only as scanned images (about 13%) could not be read, so only a list and original links are provided in the 'Scanned filings' tab. ETFs were identified from the filing’s asset type and name or ticker and may be wrong. Updated weekly.
