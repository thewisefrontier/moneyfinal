Data basis · XBRL data from 10-K annual reports filed with the SEC. Fiscal year-ends differ by company, so periods are labeled by fiscal year-end date instead of year. Amounts are in US dollars, abbreviated as K, M, B and T. If the same period was amended, the latest value is used. Industries without gross profit or operating income lines, such as banks, insurers and REITs, show '-' and because XBRL accounts differ by company, some items may be blank. Annual figures come from 10-K filings and quarterly figures from 10-Q filings (last 5 years). Coverage is S&P 500 constituents plus some stocks this site tracks.
