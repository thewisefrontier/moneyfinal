What is Saju?

Saju (四柱) means the “four pillars” of the year, month, day and hour of your birth. Each pillar is written with one Heavenly Stem and one Earthly Branch, which makes eight characters in all (Palja, 八字). The ten Heavenly Stems are Gap, Eul, Byeong, Jeong, Mu, Gi, Gyeong, Sin, Im and Gye; the twelve Earthly Branches are Ja, Chuk, In, Myo, Jin, Sa, O, Mi, Sin, Yu, Sul and Hae.

The Day Master and the five elements

Of the four pillars, the Heavenly Stem of the day you were born is called the Day Master (日干), and in Saju it stands for 'you'. Every character belongs to one of the five elements (Wood 木, Fire 火, Earth 土, Metal 金 and Water 水), and how evenly they are spread across the eight characters is a clue to your innate temperament and what you may want to balance.

How far does this reading go?

This page only reads what the Manseryeok almanac can calculate mechanically: your Day Master (personality tendencies), the distribution of the five elements and the age your Luck Cycles begin. It does not go as far as the Gyeokguk and Yongsin judgments of professional Myeongni scholars, so enjoy it for fun and reference, and do not rely on it for important decisions.

Frequently asked questions

What if I don’t know my birth hour?

If you don’t know your birth hour, check 'Time unknown'. The reading then uses three pillars (year, month and day) without the hour pillar, so it covers a little less.

Should I enter a solar or a lunar date?

Enter whichever you know. If you enter a lunar date and that month was a leap month, check 'Leap month'. The Manseryeok almanac converts between solar and lunar dates for you.
