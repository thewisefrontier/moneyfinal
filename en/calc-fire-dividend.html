⚠️ This calculator gives reference estimates and does not replace tax or health insurance advice.
· For overseas (US and other) ETFs, the dividend yield is applied automatically from actual dividend history and profiles (Dividend ETF data); for Korean ETFs there is no live data source yet, so the yield you enter is used.
· The contribution simulation assumes "price growth + dividend yield"is the total return and grows with monthly compounding (when dividends are reinvested). Actual results can vary widely with market volatility.
· If financial income (interest + dividends) exceeds ₩20 million a year, progressive comprehensive income tax rates apply to the excess. US withholding already paid on overseas dividends is approximated as a foreign tax credit. If you have other comprehensive income (such as salary), your tax bracket may differ.
· Health insurance premiums (regional subscriber) are a simplified estimate based on income only. Actual regional premiums also factor in property and vehicle scores and may be higher than this estimate. A dependent loses eligibility when financial income exceeds ₩20 million a year and switches to a regional subscriber.
· The Monte Carlo simulation assumes each year’s return follows "a normal distribution of weighted average total return (mean) ± volatility (standard deviation)"and repeats 1,000 times, withdrawing the after-tax annual take-home amount calculated above each year. "Adjust for inflation"increases withdrawals each year by the assumed inflation rate (nominal amount fixed when off).
· "Compare sequence-risk scenarios"is a simplified deterministic scenario that compares a return shock of the same size (−2 standard deviations) arriving early versus late in retirement, showing that with the same average return the final balance can differ greatly depending on when the bad year comes (separate from the actual Monte Carlo probability calculation).
· For Korean ETFs, picking from the list of 15 applies actual distribution history (Yahoo Finance); for stocks not on the list, you must enter the yield by hand with "Custom" to enter the yield manually.
