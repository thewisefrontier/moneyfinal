Purpose

MoneyFinal is an independent project that gathers scattered official filings and public financial data in one place so you can compare and analyze it for free. Our tools include ETF dividend calculators, company financial statements, side-by-side company comparisons, superinvestor 13F holdings, US House members’ stock trades and an IPO calendar, all free to use.

Operating principles

  • Free, no login: no sign-up, no personal data entry and no payments.
  • Official data first: we rely on filings and APIs officially published by regulators and public institutions such as the SEC, and we cite our sources.
  • Neutral information: we do not recommend or broker any security, financial company or product.
  • Transparent calculations: the formulas and assumptions behind each calculator are stated on the page.

Data sources
