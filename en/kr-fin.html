Korean Company Financial Statements
Select a company
Data basis · Key accounts of the consolidated financial statements in filings to Korea’s Financial Supervisory Service (DART), or separate statements where no consolidated ones exist. Amounts are in Korean won (₩) and abbreviated as K, M, B and T. Quarterly values are three-month figures from quarterly reports, and Q4 is the annual figure from the business report minus the nine-month year-to-date total. Quarterly financials are provided only for roughly the top 600 companies by market cap; the rest have annual data only. Net income is net income attributable to owners of the parent, and ROE and PER are computed from it and parent equity; for companies without it (non-December year-ends, new listings and so on), net income including non-controlling interests is used and noted in the name. Financial holding companies, banks and insurers report revenue and debt differently from ordinary companies, so these may show as '-'or look unusually large.
