Effective date: 2026-10-06
MoneyFinal does not collect, store or use users’ personal information. There is no sign-up, login or personal-data form, and using the service requires no personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect any personal information, including name, email, phone number, address, bank account number, payment information or location. We also do not issue any member identifier (ID) to identify users.

2. Information collected automatically (access logs)

Cloudflare Pages, which hosts this site, may temporarily store technical logs (visit time, part of the IP address, browser information and the like) for operation and security. These are handled under Cloudflare’s privacy policy, and we do not access them.

  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

MoneyFinal does not set its own cookies. However, your dark or light theme preference may be stored in your browser’s local storage; this is not personal information and stays only in your browser.

MoneyFinal serves ads through Google AdSense. Third-party advertisers, including Google, may use cookies and similar identifiers to serve ads based on your previous visits. MoneyFinal does not collect or store this information directly.

  • Through Google’s use of advertising cookies, Google and its partners may serve personalized ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads at Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com), and you can opt out of third-party vendors’ personalized-ad cookies at aboutads.info.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although some features may be limited.
  • You can learn how Google uses data in its advertising services at Google policies (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

The appearance of an ad does not mean MoneyFinal recommends or endorses the advertised financial product. If we introduce an analytics service (such as Google Analytics), we will describe it separately in this policy.

5. Disclosure to third parties

Because we collect no personal information, we provide none to third parties.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no personal information, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. You can still send questions about using the site to the contact below.
