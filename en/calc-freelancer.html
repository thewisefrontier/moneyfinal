💡 Which is better?
⚠️ Withholding is a prepayment, and the final tax is settled in the comprehensive income tax return filed the following May.
· Business income (3.3%): for services provided on an ongoing, repeated basis. Necessary expenses are recognized by actual spending or simplified or standard expense rates.
· Other income (8.8%): one-off or incidental income such as lecture or manuscript fees. 60% necessary expenses are recognized automatically, and if the other income amount (after expenses) is ₩50,000 or less per case (payment of ₩125,000 or less), it falls under the minimum taxable threshold and no withholding applies.
· You can choose separate taxation for other income of ₩3 million or less a year (excluded from comprehensive income).
· The income type is decided by the substance of the contract and cannot be chosen freely. Consult a tax accountant for an exact determination.
