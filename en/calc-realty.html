· Monthly rent transaction amount = = deposit + monthly rent × 100 (recalculated as monthly rent × 70 if under ₩50 million)
· Acquisition tax: 1 home 1–3% (1% up to ₩600M, sliding scale from ₩600–900M, 3% over ₩900M), 8% for 2 homes in an adjustment area, 3 homes 12% in adjustment and 8% in non-adjustment areas, 12% for 4 or more homes or corporations. A temporary 2-home owner gets the 1-home rate.
· The total includes local education tax and the special rural development tax (over 85 ㎡); reductions such as for first-time buyers are not reflected.
