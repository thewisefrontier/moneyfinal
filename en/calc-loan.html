⚠️ These are reference estimates. Actual loans vary with a bank’s daily accrual, prepayment fees, rate changes (variable rate) and grace periods.
· Equal installment: the same amount is repaid every month. Total interest is higher than equal principal, but the initial burden is lighter.
· Equal principal: the principal portion is the same each month and interest falls, so payments gradually decrease. Total interest is the lowest.
· Bullet: only interest is paid monthly and the whole principal is repaid at maturity. Total interest is the highest.
